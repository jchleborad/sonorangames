Your information

Privacy Policy

Sonoran Games is a privately managed NFL Pick’em pool. Participation is by invitation, but the website’s scoreboards, results, and other public pages can be viewed by anyone with the address.

Information used to run the pool

We retain player names or display names, player identifiers, optional email addresses, participation status, submitted picks, tiebreaker predictions, submission times, results, and administrative records. We use these records to provide access, run the competition, resolve questions, and maintain accurate history.

Display names, picks made available through Who Picked Who, results, rankings, and winner recognition may appear on public pages. Contact information and access credentials are not intended for public display.

Your personal link and this browser

Your personal player link gives access to your card. Keep it private. The site uses browser storage to remember player access and retain local pick drafts; Scores can then show your saved picks automatically. Clearing site data may remove that remembered access and local drafts. It does not remove submitted picks from the pool’s records.

Services that help the site operate

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Google services support pool records, application requests, and web fonts. The Home page uses GoatCounter for traffic statistics. These providers may process technical information when your browser connects to them under their own policies. Sonoran Games does not sell player information or use it for targeted advertising.

Provider information: GitHub Privacy Statement, Google Privacy Policy, and GoatCounter Privacy Policy.

For questions or corrections, contact the Administrator using the text or email contact already provided to your pool.

Using the site

Terms of Use

Use Sonoran Games for the pool you have been invited to join. The official pool rules govern entry, deadlines, scoring, tiebreakers, prizes, and assistance with picks. This page explains site use and support; it does not change those competition rules.

Players are responsible for using their own access link, submitting their card, and checking that their intended selections were saved. Each matchup locks at its scheduled kickoff. A highlighted choice or an unsent local draft is not a substitute for a saved submission.

Scores and standings depend on incoming data and processing and may be delayed or corrected. Report a suspected error to the Administrator. The site may occasionally be unavailable; follow the assistance process in the official rules if a technical problem prevents submission.

Sonoran Games is independently operated and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL or its teams. Team names and related marks belong to their respective owners. Original Sonoran Games content, design, and software may not be reproduced without permission.

Updates to these site policies will be dated here. Changes to competition rules require separate communication from the Administrator.

Keep it fair

Acceptable Use Rules

  • Use your own player access. Do not share personal links, impersonate another player, or enter or change someone else’s picks without authorization.
  • Do not attempt to bypass kickoff locks, access restrictions, or Administrator controls, or interfere with pool records or site operation.
  • Keep competition and communications respectful. Harassment, threats, and abusive conduct are not acceptable.
  • Do not republish another player’s contact information or access link. Report exposed links or suspected errors directly to the Administrator.

The Administrator may restrict access to protect players and the pool while reviewing misuse. Questions about scoring or eligibility are handled under the official pool rules.

Preserving pool history

Data Retention

Submitted picks, tiebreakers, results, standings, and weekly winners are retained to operate the season and preserve competition history. Submission and administrative records are retained to explain corrections and resolve questions. These records are not automatically erased when the week changes or a player becomes inactive.

Player identifiers, display names, optional contact information, participation status, and access records may remain while needed for pool administration and historical accuracy. There is currently no fixed automatic deletion schedule. Hosting and service providers manage their own logs and retention under their policies.

Browser access information and drafts may remain until cleared by the site or the person using that browser. Revoking an access link prevents its future use; it does not erase the player’s past submissions or results.

Contact the Administrator about inaccurate information or a retention concern. Requests will be reviewed with regard to the information involved and the integrity of the competition record.

When you need a hand

Admin Support Expectations

The Administrator manages the pool on a best-effort basis. Immediate responses and continuous support coverage are not guaranteed. Send questions early using the text or email contact provided to your pool.

If a technical issue prevents a pick

Send your request before the affected game kicks off. Clearly identify yourself, the matchup, and the exact selection or change requested. One request may cover multiple games that have not started. Include the error message or a screenshot if available, but keep personal access links private.

Assistance follows the official assistance rules. No pick may be added or changed after that game has begun. Requests and any Administrator action are recorded. A support request is not confirmation that a change was completed.

If a score or result looks wrong

Identify the week, game or player, and the discrepancy. The Administrator will review the relevant records and data. Reporting an error does not itself change a published result.
